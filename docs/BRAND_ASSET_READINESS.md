# SHABABUNA brand assets

The user-supplied Arabic wordmark and basketball-player mark are installed in black and white variants under `public/brand/`. The header automatically uses the English or Arabic wordmark based on the selected language. The original uploaded artwork is preserved in the package and derivative files are prepared for header, footer, favicon, PWA and social sharing.

Primary visual rules:

- Black and white dominate every page.
- Green is restricted to verified Ready to Ship and success states.
- Use wide spacing, large imagery, short headings and restrained motion.
- Do not add decorative colours, crowded cards, persistent pop-ups or excessive navigation.
- `BUILT DIFFERENT.` is the English campaign line.
